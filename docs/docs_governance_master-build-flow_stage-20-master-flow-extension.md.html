Stage 20 — Live Activation & Governance Convergence

Institutional Role

Stage 20 is the operational transition boundary between:

  • engineered trading platform
    and:
  • governed production trading infrastructure

This stage introduces:

  • semantic governance
  • repository topology governance
  • operational lineage isolation
  • deterministic orchestration governance
  • controlled live-capital activation
