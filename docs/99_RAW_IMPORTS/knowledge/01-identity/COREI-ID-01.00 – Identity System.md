# =============================================================================
#
# 1. PURPOSE
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Identity System defines the constitutional
identity of COREI.

It establishes who COREI is,
why COREI exists,
what COREI believes,
and the enduring principles that remain
constant throughout the lifetime of the institution.

The Identity System serves as the highest
institutional authority governing the identity
of COREI.



# =============================================================================
#
# 2. OBJECTIVES
#
# =============================================================================

The Identity System exists to

• define the constitutional identity of COREI

• preserve institutional continuity

• establish one authoritative identity

• guide every institutional decision

• provide a common identity for people,
  platforms, artificial intelligence,
  engineering, and operations

• ensure identity remains consistent as
  COREI evolves



# =============================================================================
#
# 3. IDENTITY PRINCIPLES
#
# =============================================================================

The Identity System follows the following
constitutional principles.

Identity precedes governance.

Governance protects identity.

Architecture expresses identity.

Engineering implements identity.

Operations execute identity.

Research expands identity without changing it.

Identity evolves only through constitutional review.

# =============================================================================

# 5. IDENTITY RESPONSIBILITIES
#
# =============================================================================

The Identity Domain owns the institutional
definition of COREI.

It defines

who COREI is,

why COREI exists,

what COREI believes,

what COREI values,

how COREI thinks,

and the long-term direction of COREI.

The Identity Domain does not define

governance,

architecture,

engineering,

implementation,

operations,

or technical specifications.

# =============================================================================
#
# 7. CONSTITUTIONAL STABILITY
#
# =============================================================================

The Identity System represents the
most stable institutional layer of COREI.

Identity shall change only when the
constitutional understanding of COREI
fundamentally changes.

Operational changes,

technology changes,

engineering changes,

market changes,

or organizational growth

shall not alter the constitutional identity
of COREI.



# =============================================================================
#
# 8. INSTITUTIONAL ROLE
#
# =============================================================================

The Identity System ensures that every
future capability developed by COREI
inherits the same institutional identity.

Whether applied to

Financial Intelligence,

Technology Intelligence,

Cyber Security,

Artificial Intelligence,

Knowledge Intelligence,

Research,

Engineering,

or future domains,

COREI remains one institution
with one constitutional identity.



# =============================================================================
#
# 9. AUTHORITY
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Identity System is the
authoritative constitutional definition
of the COREI Identity Domain.

Every Knowledge Asset within the
Identity Domain,

every Governance Asset,

every Architecture Asset,

every Engineering Asset,

every operational capability,

every AI system,

every platform,

and every future COREI initiative

shall inherit from this Identity System.

Changes to this document require
constitutional review.
