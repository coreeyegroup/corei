# =============================================================================
#
# 1. PURPOSE
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Platform Standards define the
constitutional standards governing every platform
within the COREI Institution.

Their purpose is to ensure that every platform
remains architecturally consistent,
interoperable,
secure,
observable,
scalable,
maintainable,
and constitutionally aligned regardless of
business domain or implementation technology.

Every platform shall inherit these standards.



# =============================================================================
#
# 2. PLATFORM PHILOSOPHY
#
# =============================================================================

Within COREI,

a platform is not a collection of software.

A platform is a governed institutional capability
that delivers reusable services supporting one or
more institutional domains.

Platforms shall expose capabilities.

Platforms shall not own institutional purpose.

Platforms shall evolve without compromising
constitutional consistency.



# =============================================================================
#
# 3. CONSTITUTIONAL PLATFORM STANDARDS
#
# =============================================================================

Every COREI platform shall comply with the
following constitutional standards.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 1

Identity Alignment.

Every platform shall preserve COREI Identity.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 2

Governance Compliance.

Every platform shall comply with institutional
Governance.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 3

Architectural Fidelity.

Every platform shall faithfully implement the
approved Platform Architecture.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 4

Capability Orientation.

Platforms shall expose institutional capabilities
through clearly governed services.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 5

Modularity.

Platforms shall be composed of modular,
independently maintainable capabilities.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 6

Loose Coupling.

Platform dependencies shall remain explicit,
controlled, and minimally coupled.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 7

Contract First.

Every platform interface shall be governed by
published contracts.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 8

Observability By Default.

Platforms shall expose operational metrics,
logging,
distributed tracing,
health indicators,
and audit evidence.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 9

Operational Resilience.

Platforms shall be engineered for continuous,
reliable production operation.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 10

Continuous Platform Evolution.

Platforms shall continuously improve while
preserving constitutional architecture.



# =============================================================================
#
# 4. PLATFORM STANDARD CATEGORIES
#
# =============================================================================

Platform Standards govern

platform architecture,

platform capabilities,

platform ownership,

service composition,

service communication,

API governance,

event communication,

dependency governance,

runtime governance,

deployment governance,

operational governance,

platform observability,

platform resilience,

and future platform standards.



# =============================================================================
#
# 5. PLATFORM RESPONSIBILITIES
#
# =============================================================================

Every platform shall clearly define

institutional purpose,

constitutional ownership,

business capability,

engineering ownership,

service boundaries,

contracts,

security boundaries,

data ownership,

runtime responsibilities,

operational responsibilities,

monitoring responsibilities,

and lifecycle responsibilities.

Platform ownership shall remain explicit.



# =============================================================================
#
# 6. PLATFORM COMPLIANCE
#
# =============================================================================

Every platform shall demonstrate compliance with

constitutional Identity,

Governance,

Architecture,

Engineering,

Institutional Standards,

Engineering Standards,

security requirements,

operational requirements,

quality requirements,

and production requirements.

Platform compliance shall remain measurable and
auditable.



# =============================================================================
#
# 7. PLATFORM VALIDATION
#
# =============================================================================

Every platform shall answer the following
constitutional questions.

Does it preserve COREI Identity?

Does it implement approved Architecture?

Does it expose institutional capabilities?

Are responsibilities clearly owned?

Are interfaces governed?

Are contracts published?

Are dependencies controlled?

Is observability complete?

Is operational resilience demonstrated?

Can future engineering teams safely evolve it?

If any answer is negative,

platform approval shall remain incomplete.



# =============================================================================
#
# 8. PLATFORM DECLARATION
#
# =============================================================================

COREI Platform Standards exist to ensure that
every institutional platform remains consistent,
interoperable,
reliable,
maintainable,
secure,
and continuously evolvable.

Every platform shall strengthen

institutional capability,

architectural consistency,

engineering quality,

operational excellence,

knowledge preservation,

and long-term institutional continuity.



# =============================================================================
#
# 9. AUTHORITY
#
# =============================================================================

This document defines the constitutional
Platform Standards of COREI.

Every institutional platform,

business platform,

engineering platform,

AI platform,

trading platform,

data platform,

security platform,

COREI OS,

infrastructure platform,

automation platform,

research platform,

and future platform

shall comply with these Platform Standards.

Changes to this document require constitutional
review.
