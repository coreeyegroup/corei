# =============================================================================
#
# 1. PURPOSE
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Institutional Standards establish the
constitutional engineering standards governing every
engineering activity, implementation, repository,
platform, service, workflow, automation, and future
institutional capability.

These standards preserve engineering consistency,
institutional quality, architectural integrity,
knowledge continuity, and long-term maintainability
throughout the COREI Institution.



# =============================================================================
#
# 2. ENGINEERING STANDARD PHILOSOPHY
#
# =============================================================================

Within COREI,

standards do not exist to restrict engineering.

Standards exist to preserve institutional
understanding.

Every engineering standard shall reduce ambiguity,
increase consistency, strengthen architectural
fidelity, improve engineering quality, and preserve
institutional knowledge.

Engineering freedom exists inside constitutional
boundaries.

Engineering inconsistency does not.



# =============================================================================
#
# 3. CONSTITUTIONAL ENGINEERING STANDARDS
#
# =============================================================================

Every engineering implementation shall comply with
the following constitutional standards.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 1

Identity Alignment

Every implementation shall preserve the
constitutional identity of COREI.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 2

Governance Compliance

Every engineering activity shall comply with
constitutional governance.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 3

Architecture Fidelity

Engineering shall faithfully implement approved
architecture without reinterpretation.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 4

Deterministic Engineering

Every implementation shall produce predictable,
repeatable, reproducible behaviour.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 5

Institution Before Technology

Technology choices shall support institutional
architecture rather than define it.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 6

Describe → Implement → Validate → Document

Every engineering activity shall follow the
constitutional execution sequence.

No implementation shall bypass documentation
or validation.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 7

No Patch Work

Temporary fixes shall never become institutional
solutions.

Root causes shall be identified and resolved
through constitutional engineering.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 8

Production-Grade Engineering

Every implementation shall be designed for
operational reliability from its inception.

Prototype practices shall not migrate into
production environments.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 9

Knowledge Preservation

Every engineering decision shall increase
institutional knowledge through documentation,
traceability, and learning.

------------------------------------------------

Standard 10

Continuous Improvement

Every completed engineering activity shall improve
future engineering capability.



# =============================================================================
#
# 4. IMPLEMENTATION STANDARDS
#
# =============================================================================

Every implementation shall define

constitutional purpose,

institutional ownership,

architectural dependencies,

explicit interfaces,

published contracts,

security controls,

observability,

verification criteria,

validation criteria,

operational readiness,

knowledge documentation,

and maintenance responsibilities.

Incomplete implementations are constitutionally
non-compliant.



# =============================================================================
#
# 5. REPOSITORY STANDARDS
#
# =============================================================================

Every repository shall maintain

clear ownership,

defined architectural boundaries,

modular organization,

version control discipline,

documented public interfaces,

dependency governance,

traceable engineering decisions,

automated validation,

and production readiness.

Repositories shall preserve institutional
knowledge as well as source code.



# =============================================================================
#
# 6. ENGINEERING VALIDATION
#
# =============================================================================

Every engineering implementation shall answer
the following constitutional questions.

Does it preserve Identity?

Does it comply with Governance?

Does it faithfully implement Architecture?

Does it satisfy Engineering Principles?

Has it followed the institutional lifecycle?

Has quality been engineered?

Has documentation been completed?

Can another engineering team maintain it?

Has institutional knowledge increased?

If any answer is negative,

the engineering work remains incomplete.



# =============================================================================
#
# 7. STANDARDS DECLARATION
#
# =============================================================================

COREI Institutional Standards exist to ensure
that engineering excellence remains consistent
throughout every generation of institutional
growth.

Standards shall preserve

institutional understanding,

architectural integrity,

engineering quality,

knowledge continuity,

operational reliability,

and constitutional consistency.

Standards shall continuously mature through
institutional learning.



# =============================================================================
#
# 8. AUTHORITY
#
# =============================================================================

This document defines the constitutional
Institutional Standards of COREI.

Every repository,

engineering standard,

coding standard,

implementation guide,

architecture standard,

platform,

service,

workflow,

automation,

deployment,

runtime,

and future engineering capability

shall inherit from these standards.

Changes to these standards require constitutional
review.
