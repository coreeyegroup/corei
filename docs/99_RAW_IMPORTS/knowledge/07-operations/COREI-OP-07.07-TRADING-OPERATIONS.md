# 1. PURPOSE

#

# =============================================================================

The COREI Trading Operations document defines the
authoritative institutional operating model for
running,
controlling,
observing,
and recovering the complete trading lifecycle.

Its purpose is to ensure that trading activity
remains

authorized,

deterministic,

risk-controlled,

portfolio-aware,

broker-aware,

account-aware,

market-aware,

observable,

reconcilable,

recoverable,

and institutionally accountable.

Trading Operations SHALL preserve the complete
authority chain from market observation to final
financial state.

# =============================================================================

#

# 2. SCOPE

#

# =============================================================================

This document governs operational trading across

Forex,

Commodities,

Crypto,

Equities,

Indices,

Derivatives,

Options,

Futures,

and future approved asset classes.

It governs

market intelligence,

signal intelligence,

decision intelligence,

risk intelligence,

portfolio intelligence,

execution intelligence,

position intelligence,

performance intelligence,

system intelligence,

accounts,

brokers,

strategies,

portfolios,

assets,

markets,

regions,

currencies,

custodians,

exchanges,

jurisdictions,

operators,

automation,

and artificial intelligence systems.

#

# =============================================================================

generate signals,

form decisions,

approve risk,

allocate capital,

authorize execution,

submit orders,

cancel orders,

modify orders,

close positions,

suspend trading,

and resume trading.

trading authority.

# =============================================================================

explicit ownership.

risk,

execution,

incident response,

reconciliation,

and recovery.
