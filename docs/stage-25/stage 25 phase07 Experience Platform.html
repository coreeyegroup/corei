01 Executive Summary

Phase‑07 implements the institutional Experience Platform – the highest operational abstraction below the COREI Operating System. An Experience represents a complete operational environment (Trading, Infrastructure, Risk, Research, Executive, Compliance, etc.) that assembles Workspaces, Capabilities, Providers, and Runtime services into a cohesive operator-facing environment. The platform includes governance layers (Foundation, Lifecycle, State, Composition, Runtime, Integrations, Runtime Graph, Runtime Orchestrator) and two concrete experience implementations (Trading, Infrastructure).
