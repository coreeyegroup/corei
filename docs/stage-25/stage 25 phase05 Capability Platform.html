01 Executive Summary

Phase‑05 implements the institutional Capability Platform – the business abstraction layer that sits between the Provider Platform and all higher-level operational platforms. Capabilities represent what COREI can do (e.g., Chart, Terminal, Grid, Editor, Workspace) independent of the underlying providers. The platform consists of a generic foundation, lifecycle, state, dependency management, and four specialized capability families: UI, Workspace, Visualization, and Productivity. This platform ensures vendor independence and deterministic business abstraction.
