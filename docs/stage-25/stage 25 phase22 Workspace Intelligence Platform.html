01 Executive Summary

Phase‑22 delivers the Workspace Intelligence Platform – the institutional context‑aware intelligence layer. It consumes the completed Platform Kernel (Phases 01‑16), Institutional Shell (Phase‑17), Capability Provider Platform (Phase‑18), Window & Workspace Platform (Phase‑19), Panel & Visualization Platform (Phase‑20), and Operator Productivity Platform (Phase‑21), and operationalises existing intelligence, context, state, and event foundations – without rebuilding them.

The phase follows the Platform Enablement philosophy:

Consume → Integrate → Coordinate → Operationalize → Validate → Freeze

The authoritative execution map defines 21 controlled steps (see §19). At completion, Phase‑22 provides:

  • ✅ Intelligence Runtime (Singleton, single entry point)
  • ✅ Intelligence Services (context-aware insight/recommendation generation)
  • ✅ Intelligence Context (workspace-aware context coordination)
  • ✅ Intelligence State (insight/recommendation state management)
  • ✅ Intelligence Event Integration (event‑driven intelligence refresh)
  • ✅ Multi‑Workspace Intelligence Coordination
  • ✅ Panel, Visualization, and Operator Productivity Integration
  • ✅ Diagnostics & Observability
  • ✅ Intelligence Right Panel and Workspace Intelligence Panel
  • ✅ Shell consumption and browser validation

Current Status: Phase‑22 is certified complete and frozen. All 21 steps have been implemented, validated, and the repository is ready for Phase‑23 consumption.

03 Architecture

The Phase‑22 architecture follows the institutional layered model. It is organised into five execution blocks:

  1. Repository Truth (Steps 01–03) – baseline discovery, contract boundary, domain & ownership certification.
  2. Backend Intelligence Foundations (Steps 04–08) – runtime, lifecycle, context/state, services, event integration.
  3. Platform Integrations (Steps 09–12) – platform composition, panel, visualization, operator productivity integration.
  4. Multi‑Workspace & Experience (Steps 13–15) – multi‑workspace coordination, backend composition, frontend workspace experience.
  5. Validation, Audit & Freeze (Steps 16–21) – shell consumption, diagnostics, platform validation, audit, certification, freeze.

The target composition chain is:

Institutional Shell
    ↓
Workspace Platform
    ↓
Panel Platform / Visualization Platform / Operator Productivity Platform
    ↓
Workspace Intelligence Platform
    │
    ├── Intelligence Runtime (Singleton)
    ├── Intelligence Services
    ├── Intelligence Context
    ├── Intelligence State
    ├── Intelligence Event Integration
    ├── Multi-Workspace Coordination
    └── Diagnostics & Observability
    │
    ▼
Intelligence Right Panel / Workspace Intelligence Panel
    │
    ▼
Operator Experience

Intelligence ownership remains with Phase‑22; all consumed platforms are preserved without modification.

19 Appendix

A. Phase‑22 21‑Step Execution Map

  1. Phase Entry, Repository Reality & Architecture Reconciliation – Baseline discovery; certification of existing intelligence capabilities.
  2. Workspace Intelligence Platform Contract Boundary – Platform‑level contracts – no runtime.
  3. Intelligence Domain & Ownership Certification – Domain reconciliation; public/private boundaries.
  4. Intelligence Runtime Coordination – Singleton Runtime – single intelligence entry point.
  5. Intelligence Lifecycle Integration – Workspace lifecycle integration (initialize, activate, refresh, suspend, resume, dispose).
  6. Intelligence State & Context Coordination – Workspace-aware context and insight/recommendation state.
  7. Intelligence Service Integration – Service layer for insight and recommendation generation.
  8. Intelligence Event Integration – Event‑driven intelligence refresh (consumes Event Platform).
  9. Workspace Intelligence Composition – Compose Runtime, Services, Context, State, Events into unified platform.
  10. Panel Intelligence Consumption – Integrate Intelligence with Panel Platform.
  11. Visualization Intelligence Integration – Make Intelligence available to Visualization Platform.
  12. Operator Productivity Intelligence Integration – Make Intelligence available to Command Palette, Search, Monaco, Terminal.
  13. Multi‑Workspace Intelligence Coordination – Per‑workspace intelligence context and isolation.
  14. Backend Intelligence Platform Composition – Compose complete backend platform boundary.
  15. Frontend Intelligence Workspace Experience – Institutional Intelligence experience (Right Panel, Panel).
  16. Shell Consumption & Browser Validation – Validate Shell consumption and browser integration.
  17. Intelligence Diagnostics & Observability – Runtime health, service diagnostics, context/state/event diagnostics.
  18. Platform Integration Validation – End‑to‑end integration validation.
  19. Repository & Architecture Audit – Formal repository and architecture audit.
  20. Phase Certification – Formal certification.
  21. Controlled Freeze & Consumption Handoff – Freeze and handoff to Phase‑23.

Critical rule: Each step's implementation scope is defined by the phase document; repository reconciliation determines the exact implementation delta within that scope, not the step sequence.
