01 Executive Summary

Phase‑23 delivers the Identity & Organization Platform – the institutional identity and organizational context layer. It consumes the completed Platform Kernel (Phases 01‑16), Institutional Shell (Phase‑17), Capability Provider Platform (Phase‑18), Window & Workspace Platform (Phase‑19), Panel & Visualization Platform (Phase‑20), Operator Productivity Platform (Phase‑21), and Workspace Intelligence Platform (Phase‑22), and operationalises existing identity, organization, role, and permission foundations – without rebuilding them.

The phase follows the Platform Enablement philosophy:

Consume → Integrate → Coordinate → Operationalize → Validate → Freeze

The authoritative execution map defines 21 controlled steps (see §19). At completion, Phase‑23 provides:

  • ✅ Identity Runtime (Singleton, single entry point)
  • ✅ Identity Services (Identity, Organization, Role, Permission services)
  • ✅ Identity State and Context (unified identity context)
  • ✅ Identity Event Integration (event‑driven identity refresh)
  • ✅ Organization Hierarchy (Institution → Organization → Business Unit → Department → Team → Operator)
  • ✅ Role & Permission Coordination (runtime authorization context)
  • ✅ Shell Identity Integration (IdentityProvider wraps Shell)
  • ✅ Workspace Identity Integration (identity bound to workspace)
  • ✅ Identity UI Components (OrganizationDisplay, RoleDisplay, IdentityProvider, hooks)
  • ✅ Diagnostics & Observability

Current Status: Phase‑23 is certified complete and frozen. The repository already contains a substantial identity implementation – all 21 steps have been implemented, validated, and the platform is ready for Phase‑24 consumption.

03 Architecture

The Phase‑23 architecture follows the institutional layered model. It is organised into five execution blocks:

  1. Repository Truth (Steps 01–03) – baseline discovery, contract boundary, domain & ownership certification.
  2. Backend Identity Foundations (Steps 04–08) – runtime, lifecycle, state/context, services, event integration.
  3. Platform Integrations (Steps 09–13) – platform composition, shell, workspace, organization, role & permission integration.
  4. Frontend Experience (Steps 14–15) – backend platform composition, frontend identity experience.
  5. Validation, Audit & Freeze (Steps 16–21) – shell consumption, diagnostics, platform validation, audit, certification, freeze.

The target composition chain is:

Platform Kernel
    ↓
Institutional Shell (Phase‑17)
    ↓
Workspace Platform (Phase‑19)
    ↓
Panel & Visualization Platform (Phase‑20)
    ↓

19 Appendix

A. Phase‑23 21‑Step Execution Map

  1. Repository Reality & Identity Discovery – Baseline discovery; certification of existing identity capabilities.
  2. Identity Platform Contract Boundary – Platform‑level contracts – no runtime.
  3. Identity Domain & Ownership Certification – Domain reconciliation; public/private boundaries.
  4. Identity Runtime Coordination – Singleton Runtime – single identity entry point.
  5. Identity Lifecycle Integration – Workspace lifecycle integration (initialize, activate, refresh, suspend, resume, dispose).
  6. Identity State & Context Coordination – Identity context (institution, organization, operator, role, permission).
  7. Identity Service Integration – Service layer for identity, organization, role, permission.
  8. Identity Event Integration – Event‑driven identity refresh (consumes Event Platform).
  9. Identity Platform Composition – Compose Runtime, Services, Context, State, Events into unified platform.
  10. Shell Identity Integration – Integrate Identity Platform with Institutional Shell (IdentityProvider wraps Shell).
  11. Workspace Identity Integration – Bind identity context to active workspace.
  12. Organization & Team Integration – Organization hierarchy (Institution → Organization → Business Unit → Department → Team).
  13. Role & Permission Coordination – Role and effective permission context.
  14. Backend Identity Platform Composition – Compose complete backend platform boundary.
  15. Frontend Identity Experience – Institutional identity experience (Identity Provider, hooks, components).
  16. Shell Consumption & Browser Validation – Validate Shell consumption and browser integration.
  17. Identity Diagnostics & Observability – Runtime health, context integrity, organization/role/permission health.
  18. Platform Integration Validation – End‑to‑end integration validation.
  19. Repository & Architecture Audit – Formal repository and architecture audit.
  20. Phase Certification – Formal certification.
  21. Controlled Freeze & Consumption Handoff – Freeze and handoff to Phase‑24.

Critical rule: Each step's implementation scope is defined by the phase document; repository reconciliation determines the exact implementation delta within that scope, not the step sequence.
