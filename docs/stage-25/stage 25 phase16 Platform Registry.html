01 Executive Summary

Phase‑16 implements the Platform Registry Platform – the authoritative ownership layer responsible for deterministic registration, discovery, governance, indexing, lookup, lifecycle management, and orchestration of all platform-level registries across the COREI Operating System. Unlike the Platform Configuration Platform, which governs how the platform is configured, the Platform Registry Platform governs what platform components exist and how they are discovered and managed. This is the final phase of the Platform Kernel, completing the transition from kernel construction to kernel operational readiness.
