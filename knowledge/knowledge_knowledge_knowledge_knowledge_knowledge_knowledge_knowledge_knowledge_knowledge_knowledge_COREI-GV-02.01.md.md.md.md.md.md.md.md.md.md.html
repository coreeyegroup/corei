COREI GOVERNANCE MODEL

DOCUMENT ID

COREI-GV-02.01

DOCUMENT NAME

GOVERNANCE MODEL

VERSION

1.0

STATUS

APPROVED

CLASSIFICATION

CONSTITUTIONAL

1. PURPOSE

The COREI Governance Model defines the conceptual
model through which governance is understood and
applied across the COREI Institution.

It establishes the relationship between knowledge,
intelligence, authority, decision making,
accountability, and institutional mastery.

Every governance capability within COREI shall
inherit from this model.

2. GOVERNANCE DEFINITION

Within COREI,

governance is the disciplined stewardship of
institutional understanding.

Governance exists to ensure that knowledge is
correct,

intelligence is trustworthy,

decisions are responsible,

authority is accountable,

and institutional evolution remains aligned with
COREI’s constitutional identity.

Governance transforms institutional understanding
into responsible institutional action.

3. THE GOVERNANCE HIERARCHY

Governance within COREI follows a constitutional
hierarchy.

Reality

↓

Observation

↓

Knowledge

↓

Understanding

↓

Intelligence

↓

Governance

↓

Decision

↓

Execution

↓

Learning

↓

Institutional Mastery

Each layer depends upon the integrity of the
layer above it.

Governance without understanding is prohibited.

4. GOVERNANCE COMPONENTS

The Governance Model consists of six
constitutional components.

Knowledge

Provides institutional understanding.

Intelligence

Transforms understanding into insight.

Authority

Provides legitimate institutional power.

Decision

Selects responsible action.

Accountability

Accepts responsibility for action.

Continuous Improvement

Strengthens governance through learning.

Together these components define institutional
governance within COREI.

5. GOVERNANCE FLOW

Governance follows a deterministic lifecycle.

Acquire Knowledge

↓

Develop Understanding

↓

Generate Intelligence

↓

Evaluate Decisions

↓

Exercise Authority

↓

Execute Responsibly

↓

Measure Outcomes

↓

Capture Learning

↓

Improve Governance

This lifecycle repeats continuously
throughout the institution.

6. GOVERNANCE CHARACTERISTICS

Governance within COREI shall always be

Knowledge-driven.

Evidence-based.

Explainable.

Accountable.

Transparent.

Traceable.

Disciplined.

Institutionally responsible.

Continuously improving.

These characteristics distinguish governance
from administration or control.

7. GOVERNANCE SCOPE

The Governance Model applies to every
institutional capability.

Knowledge Governance

Identity Governance

Architecture Governance

Engineering Governance

AI Governance

Platform Governance

Operational Governance

Security Governance

Risk Governance

Research Governance

Future governance domains shall inherit this
same constitutional model.

8. GOVERNANCE OBJECTIVE

The objective of governance is not to control
institutional activity.

Its objective is to ensure that every
institutional capability continuously aligns
with

constitutional identity,

institutional knowledge,

institutional intelligence,

institutional responsibility,

and long-term institutional mastery.

9. MODEL STATEMENT

The COREI Governance Model defines governance
as the disciplined stewardship of institutional
understanding.

Governance transforms knowledge into responsible
authority,

authority into disciplined decisions,

decisions into accountable execution,

and execution into continuous institutional
learning.

Governance therefore preserves institutional
identity while enabling institutional evolution.

10. AUTHORITY

The COREI Governance Model is the authoritative
conceptual model for institutional governance.

Every Governance Principle,

Governance Framework,

Authority Model,

Decision Model,

Risk Model,

Ethics Framework,

Architecture Governance,

Engineering Governance,

Operational Governance,

and future governance capability

shall inherit from this model.

Changes to this document require constitutional
review.
