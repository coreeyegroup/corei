COREI KNOWLEDGE ASSET STANDARD

DOCUMENT ID

COREI-KS-00.07

DOCUMENT NAME

KNOWLEDGE ASSET STANDARD

VERSION

1.0

STATUS

APPROVED

CLASSIFICATION

CONSTITUTIONAL

1. PURPOSE

The COREI Knowledge Asset Standard defines the mandatory
contract that every Knowledge Asset within the COREI
Knowledge System shall follow.

The purpose of this standard is to ensure that every
Knowledge Asset remains consistent,
traceable,
governed,
machine-readable,
and institutionally reusable.

This standard applies to every Knowledge Domain.

2. OBJECTIVES

The Knowledge Asset Standard exists to

• standardize every Knowledge Asset

• establish one document contract

• support deterministic navigation

• support AI-native interpretation

• enable institutional traceability

• eliminate structural inconsistency

• preserve repository quality

• enable long-term maintainability

3. KNOWLEDGE ASSET DEFINITION

A Knowledge Asset is the smallest authoritative unit of
institutional knowledge within COREI.

Every Knowledge Asset owns one institutional
responsibility.

Knowledge Assets collectively form the COREI
Knowledge System.

4. MANDATORY METADATA

Every Knowledge Asset shall define the following metadata.

asset_id

title

domain

domain_code

document_number

classification

authority

version

status

owner

repository

depends_on

supersedes

superseded_by

review_cycle (optional)

tags (optional)

No mandatory field may be omitted.

5. KNOWLEDGE ASSET IDENTIFIER

Every Knowledge Asset shall have one globally unique
identifier.

Standard format

COREI--

Examples

COREI-ID-01.04

COREI-GV-02.03

COREI-AR-03.12

COREI-EN-04.09

Knowledge Asset identifiers are permanent.

Identifiers shall never be reused.

6. DOCUMENT STRUCTURE

Every Knowledge Asset shall follow a consistent structure.

Metadata

↓

Document Header

↓

Purpose

↓

Objectives

↓

Body

↓

Authority

↓

End of Document

Domain-specific sections may be added where required.

The standard structure shall remain consistent.

7. OWNERSHIP

Every Knowledge Asset shall have

one owner,

one primary purpose,

one authoritative location,

and one governing Knowledge Domain.

Multiple ownership is prohibited.

8. RELATIONSHIPS

Knowledge Assets shall explicitly declare relationships.

Supported relationships include

Depends On

Inherited From

Referenced By

Supersedes

Superseded By

Supports

Extends

Constrained By

Hidden relationships are prohibited.

9. VERSIONING

Every Knowledge Asset shall maintain version history.

Major Version

Constitutional or structural change.

Minor Version

Functional enhancement.

Patch Version

Editorial correction.

Version history shall remain permanently traceable.

10. LIFECYCLE STATUS

Every Knowledge Asset shall define one lifecycle status.

Draft

Review

Approved

Frozen

Deprecated

Superseded

Status shall appear within the metadata.

11. QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Every Knowledge Asset shall be

Accurate.

Complete.

Consistent.

Explainable.

Traceable.

Governed.

Version Controlled.

Machine Readable.

Institutionally Maintainable.

Knowledge Assets failing these requirements
shall not become authoritative.

12. AI COMPATIBILITY

Every Knowledge Asset shall support

Semantic retrieval.

Knowledge graph construction.

Dependency analysis.

Institutional reasoning.

Cross-domain navigation.

Machine interpretation.

The document contract shall remain deterministic
to enable reliable AI consumption.

13. AUTHORITY

The COREI Knowledge Asset Standard defines the
mandatory contract for every Knowledge Asset
within the COREI Knowledge System.

Every Knowledge Domain,

every Knowledge Asset,

every future document,

every engineering team,

every AI system,

and every automation workflow

shall comply with this standard.

Compliance with this standard is mandatory.

END OF DOCUMENT
