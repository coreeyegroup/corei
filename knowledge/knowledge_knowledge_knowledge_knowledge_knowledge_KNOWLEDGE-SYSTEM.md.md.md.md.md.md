# 1. INTRODUCTION

The COREI Knowledge System is the institutional intelligence
foundation of COREI.

It provides the authoritative structure through which
institutional knowledge is created, organized, governed,
preserved, expanded and operationalized.

The Knowledge System is designed for both humans and
artificial intelligence.

It ensures that knowledge remains deterministic,
traceable, reusable and continuously evolving.



# 2. OBJECTIVES

The COREI Knowledge System exists to

• Preserve institutional knowledge

• Prevent knowledge loss

• Eliminate duplicated knowledge

• Provide one authoritative source of truth

• Enable AI-native knowledge retrieval

• Support long-term institutional continuity

• Support engineering, governance and operations

• Transform knowledge into institutional intelligence



# 3. KNOWLEDGE PRINCIPLES

The COREI Knowledge System follows the following principles.

Knowledge belongs to exactly one owner.

Knowledge exists in exactly one authoritative location.

Knowledge is version controlled.

Knowledge is reviewed before approval.

Knowledge continuously evolves.

Identity evolves slowly.

Architecture evolves carefully.

Engineering evolves continuously.

Operations evolve daily.

History never changes.

Research never becomes authoritative until approved.



# 4. KNOWLEDGE ARCHITECTURE

The repository is organized into institutional knowledge
domains.

```
knowledge/

01-identity/
02-governance/
03-architecture/
04-engineering/
05-standards/
06-specifications/
07-operations/
08-implementation/
09-reference/
10-history/
11-research/
```

Each domain owns one institutional responsibility.

Responsibilities shall never overlap.



# 5. KNOWLEDGE HIERARCHY

Institutional knowledge is organized from
highest authority to lowest authority.


Knowledge may inherit downward.

Knowledge shall never inherit upward.



# 6. KNOWLEDGE OWNERSHIP

Every Knowledge Asset shall define

• Purpose

• Scope

• Owner

• Authority

• Version

• Classification

• Dependencies

• Review Status

No document shall own multiple institutional responsibilities.

# 9. AI CONSUMPTION

The Knowledge System shall be optimized for

Human understanding.

Machine understanding.

Semantic retrieval.

Institutional reasoning.

The Knowledge System shall continuously evolve.

New knowledge shall strengthen existing knowledge.

Knowledge shall never fragment institutional identity.
