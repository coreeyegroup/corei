# =============================================================================
#
# 1. PURPOSE
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Business Architecture defines the
constitutional architecture governing how the
COREI Institution creates, delivers, preserves,
and continuously compounds institutional value.

Business Architecture aligns institutional purpose,
knowledge, intelligence, governance, capabilities,
and execution into one coherent institutional system.

Business exists to fulfill the Mission of COREI.

It does not define the Mission.



# =============================================================================
#
# 2. BUSINESS PHILOSOPHY
#
# =============================================================================

Within COREI,

business is not the exchange of products
or services.

Business is the disciplined transformation of
institutional understanding into sustainable value.

The primary value created by COREI is not software,
technology, or financial products.

The primary value created by COREI is deeper
understanding of the Unseen Core governing complex
systems.

Everything else is an expression of that
understanding.



# =============================================================================
#
# 3. BUSINESS ARCHITECTURE MODEL

# =============================================================================
