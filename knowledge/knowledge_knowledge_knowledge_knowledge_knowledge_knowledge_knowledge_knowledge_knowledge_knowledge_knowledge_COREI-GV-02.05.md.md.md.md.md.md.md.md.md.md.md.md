# =============================================================================
#
# 1. PURPOSE
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Authority Model defines the constitutional
source, structure, delegation, and exercise of
authority throughout the COREI Institution.

Authority exists to enable responsible institutional
governance.

Authority shall never exist independently of
constitutional identity, institutional knowledge,
and governance.



# =============================================================================
#
# 2. AUTHORITY PHILOSOPHY
#
# =============================================================================

Within COREI,

authority is not ownership.

Authority is not hierarchy.

Authority is not privilege.

Authority is the constitutional responsibility to
make, approve, or govern institutional decisions
within defined boundaries.

Authority exists to protect the institution,
not the individual.



# =============================================================================
#
# 3. SOURCE OF AUTHORITY
#
# =============================================================================

Institutional authority originates from the
constitutional foundations of COREI.

The authority hierarchy is

COREI Identity

↓

COREI Governance

↓

Institutional Knowledge

↓

Institutional Understanding

↓

Governance Responsibility

↓

Delegated Authority

↓

Institutional Decision

↓

Institutional Accountability

Authority derives legitimacy from the
constitutional layers above it.

No authority shall exist outside this hierarchy.



# =============================================================================
#
# 4. AUTHORITY PRINCIPLES
#
# =============================================================================

Every exercise of authority shall comply with
the following constitutional principles.

Authority follows responsibility.

Authority requires accountability.

Authority requires validated knowledge.

Authority shall remain explainable.

Authority shall remain traceable.

Authority shall preserve constitutional identity.

Authority shall strengthen institutional capability.

Authority shall never exceed its constitutional scope.



# =============================================================================
#
# 5. AUTHORITY LEVELS
#
# =============================================================================

Authority is exercised through five institutional
levels.

Constitutional Authority

Owns constitutional identity,
governance,
and institutional continuity.

Strategic Authority

Owns long-term institutional direction,
major investments,
and strategic capability development.

Architectural Authority

Owns institutional structures,
platform architecture,
and technical direction.

Operational Authority

Owns operational execution,
runtime governance,
and service delivery.

Adaptive Authority

Owns continuous improvement,
optimization,
learning,
and institutional evolution.

Each level operates only within its defined scope.



# =============================================================================
#
# 6. AUTHORITY DELEGATION
#
# =============================================================================

Authority may be delegated.

Responsibility may be delegated.

Execution may be delegated.

Constitutional accountability shall never be delegated.

Delegated authority shall

define its scope,

define its limits,

define its duration,

define its responsibilities,

and remain auditable.

Delegation shall never weaken institutional governance.



# =============================================================================
#
# 7. AUTHORITY VALIDATION
#
# =============================================================================

Before exercising authority,

the following questions shall be answered.

Is this authority constitutionally valid?

Is the scope clearly defined?

Is supporting knowledge authoritative?

Is accountability explicit?

Is the decision explainable?

Does this preserve COREI Identity?

Does this strengthen institutional capability?

If any answer is negative,

authority shall not be exercised.



# =============================================================================
#
# 8. AUTHORITY CONTINUITY
#
# =============================================================================

Institutional authority shall survive

organizational change,

technology change,

leadership succession,

engineering evolution,

platform modernization,

and future institutional growth.

Authority belongs to the institution,
not to individuals.



# =============================================================================
#
# 9. AUTHORITY DECLARATION
#
# =============================================================================

COREI exercises authority through disciplined
institutional governance.

Authority exists to transform constitutional
understanding into responsible institutional action.

Authority shall always remain accountable,
traceable,
knowledge-driven,
and aligned with the constitutional identity
of COREI.

No authority shall exist beyond the boundaries
defined by this constitutional model.



# =============================================================================
#
# 10. AUTHORITY
#
# =============================================================================

This document defines the constitutional
Authority Model of COREI.

Every Governance Process,

Decision Authority,

Architecture Authority,

Engineering Authority,

Operational Authority,

Artificial Intelligence Authority,

Research Authority,

and future institutional authority

shall inherit from this model.

Changes to this document require
constitutional review.
