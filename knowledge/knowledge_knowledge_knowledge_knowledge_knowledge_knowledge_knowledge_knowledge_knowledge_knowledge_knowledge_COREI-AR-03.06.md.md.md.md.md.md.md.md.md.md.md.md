# 1. PURPOSE
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Intelligence Architecture defines the
constitutional architecture governing how
institutional knowledge is transformed into
institutional intelligence, decision capability,
institutional learning, and long-term mastery.

Intelligence Architecture exists to ensure that
COREI continuously discovers, understands,
models, governs, and masters the Unseen Core
of increasingly complex systems.



# =============================================================================
#
# 2. INTELLIGENCE PHILOSOPHY
#
# =============================================================================

Within COREI,

intelligence is not information.

Intelligence is not computation.

Intelligence is not prediction.

Intelligence is the disciplined institutional
ability to

understand,

reason,

explain,

predict,

decide,

learn,

and continuously improve

based upon validated knowledge.

Artificial Intelligence is one implementation
of Institutional Intelligence.

Institutional Intelligence is broader than AI.
