# =============================================================================
#
# 1. PURPOSE
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Knowledge Governance framework establishes the
authority, ownership, accountability, review,
approval and change management processes governing
every Knowledge Asset within the COREI Knowledge System.

Its purpose is to preserve institutional integrity,
prevent knowledge drift,
maintain one authoritative source of truth,
and ensure that institutional knowledge evolves
through disciplined governance.



# =============================================================================
#
# 2. GOVERNANCE OBJECTIVES
#
# =============================================================================

Knowledge Governance exists to

• preserve institutional integrity

• define ownership

• establish accountability

• govern knowledge evolution

• ensure constitutional compliance

• maintain repository quality

• prevent conflicting knowledge

• preserve long-term institutional continuity



# =============================================================================
#
# 3. GOVERNANCE PRINCIPLES
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Knowledge System follows these
constitutional governance principles.

Knowledge has one authoritative owner.

Knowledge has one authoritative location.

Authority follows defined governance.

Every change shall be reviewed.

Every approval shall be traceable.

Knowledge continuously evolves.

Institutional continuity shall always be preserved.

Constitutional Knowledge Assets require
constitutional review before modification.



# =============================================================================
#
# 4. GOVERNANCE AUTHORITY
#
# =============================================================================

Knowledge authority is hierarchical.

Knowledge Foundation

↓

Knowledge Domains

↓

Knowledge Assets

↓

Knowledge Content

Higher authority governs lower authority.

Lower authority shall not contradict
higher authority.

Conflicts shall be resolved by the
higher governing authority.



# =============================================================================
#
# 5. KNOWLEDGE OWNERSHIP
#
# =============================================================================

Every Knowledge Asset shall define

Asset Owner

Authority Level

Responsible Domain

Review Authority

Approval Authority

Repository Location

Ownership shall remain explicit.

Shared ownership of a Knowledge Asset
is prohibited.

Institutional collaboration is encouraged.

Institutional ownership remains singular.



# =============================================================================
#
# 6. CHANGE GOVERNANCE
#
# =============================================================================

Every proposed change shall follow
the same governance process.

Proposal

↓

Impact Analysis

↓

Institutional Review

↓

Approval

↓

Repository Update

↓

Version Increment

↓

Publication

↓

Traceability Update

Knowledge shall never be modified
without governance.



# =============================================================================
#
# 7. REVIEW LEVELS
#
# =============================================================================

Knowledge Assets shall be reviewed
according to their authority.

Constitutional

Highest institutional review.

Foundational

Cross-domain review.

Authoritative

Domain review.

Normative

Standards review.

Operational

Operational review.

Reference

Editorial review.

Historical

Historical preservation review.

Research

Research validation review.



# =============================================================================
#
# 8. KNOWLEDGE STATUS
#
# =============================================================================

Every Knowledge Asset shall have
exactly one lifecycle status.

Draft

Under development.

Review

Under institutional review.

Approved

Authoritative.

Frozen

Stable and protected.

Deprecated

No longer recommended.

Superseded

Replaced by another Knowledge Asset.

Status shall be visible
within every Knowledge Asset.



# =============================================================================
#
# 9. VERSION GOVERNANCE
#
# =============================================================================

Every Knowledge Asset shall maintain
version history.

Major Version

Constitutional or structural changes.

Minor Version

Functional improvements.

Patch Version

Editorial corrections.

Version history shall remain
fully traceable.

Historical versions shall never
be overwritten.



# =============================================================================
#
# 10. CONSTITUTIONAL PROTECTION
#
# =============================================================================

The following Knowledge Assets
require constitutional review
before modification.

Knowledge Foundation

Identity

Governance

Knowledge Architecture

Knowledge Model

Constitutional Principles

Institutional Doctrine

Changes to constitutional knowledge
shall require exceptional justification.

Institutional stability takes precedence
over convenience.



# =============================================================================
#
# 11. COMPLIANCE
#
# =============================================================================

Every Knowledge Asset shall comply with

Knowledge Model

Knowledge System

Knowledge Architecture

Knowledge Governance

Knowledge Lifecycle

Knowledge Classification

Knowledge Asset Standard

Knowledge Naming Standard

Knowledge Repository Standard

Non-compliant Knowledge Assets
shall be reviewed before approval.



# =============================================================================
#
# 12. AUTHORITY
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Knowledge Governance framework
is the authoritative governance model
for every Knowledge Asset.

It defines how institutional knowledge
is owned,
reviewed,
approved,
versioned,
protected,
and continuously evolved.

Every future contributor,
AI system,
engineering team,
automation workflow,
and operational platform
shall comply with this governance framework.

This governance framework shall remain
stable throughout the lifetime of COREI.
