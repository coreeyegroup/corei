1. PURPOSE

The COREI Knowledge Lifecycle defines the complete
institutional lifecycle of every Knowledge Asset.

It establishes how knowledge is discovered,
validated,
approved,
maintained,
evolved,
deprecated,
and historically preserved.

Every Knowledge Asset shall follow this lifecycle.

2. LIFECYCLE OBJECTIVES

The Knowledge Lifecycle exists to

• capture institutional discoveries

• eliminate knowledge loss

• ensure structured evolution

• preserve institutional continuity

• govern knowledge maturity

• maintain historical traceability

• support continuous institutional learning

3. KNOWLEDGE ORIGINS

Knowledge may originate from

Reality

Observation

Research

Engineering

Architecture

Operations

Implementation

Review

Audit

Artificial Intelligence

External Sources

Institutional Experience

Regardless of origin,
every knowledge item shall enter
the same institutional lifecycle.

Every stage strengthens
institutional intelligence.

5. KNOWLEDGE MATURITY

Knowledge continuously matures.

↓

↓

↓

↓

Institutional

Knowledge maturity shall be
visible throughout its lifecycle.

6. KNOWLEDGE EVOLUTION

Knowledge continuously evolves.

Evolution shall

improve clarity,

increase understanding,

expand capability,

eliminate ambiguity,

strengthen institutional consistency,

and preserve constitutional identity.

Knowledge shall never evolve
through uncontrolled change.

7. KNOWLEDGE MAINTENANCE

Every Knowledge Asset shall be
continuously maintained.

Maintenance activities include

Review

Correction

Expansion

Clarification

Version Update

Dependency Update

Reference Validation

Historical Recording

Maintenance shall preserve
institutional integrity.

8. KNOWLEDGE DEPRECATION

Knowledge may become deprecated.

Deprecation shall occur only when

knowledge becomes obsolete,

knowledge is superseded,

institutional direction changes,

or constitutional review approves replacement.

Deprecated Knowledge Assets
shall remain historically available.

Knowledge shall never be deleted
without institutional authorization.

9. KNOWLEDGE PRESERVATION

Institutional knowledge shall be preserved.

Historical versions

remain accessible.

Version history

remains traceable.

Institutional decisions

remain auditable.

Knowledge lineage

shall never be lost.

Institutional memory
shall continuously grow.

10. KNOWLEDGE TRANSITIONS

Knowledge transitions shall be governed.

Allowed transitions include

Draft

↓

Review

↓

Approved

↓

Frozen

↓

Deprecated

↓

Superseded

Transitions shall be recorded.

Unauthorized transitions
are prohibited.

11. LIFECYCLE PRINCIPLES

The COREI Knowledge Lifecycle follows
the following constitutional principles.

Knowledge shall always evolve.

Institutional continuity shall be preserved.

Historical knowledge shall never be destroyed.

Knowledge lineage shall remain traceable.

Knowledge quality shall continuously improve.

Institutional intelligence shall continuously expand.

12. AUTHORITY

The COREI Knowledge Lifecycle defines
the institutional lifecycle of every
Knowledge Asset within COREI.

Every contributor,

every engineer,

every architect,

every AI system,

every operational workflow,

and every future COREI platform

shall follow this lifecycle.

The lifecycle is mandatory
throughout the COREI Knowledge System.
