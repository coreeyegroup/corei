1. PURPOSE

The COREI Standards Model defines the constitutional
model through which institutional standards are
created, organized, governed, applied, validated,
and continuously evolved.

It establishes how standards preserve constitutional
consistency throughout every institutional capability.

Every institutional standard shall inherit from this
model.

2. STANDARDS DEFINITION

Within COREI,

a standard is an authoritative institutional rule
that defines the acceptable and expected manner in
which constitutional architecture, engineering,
platforms, systems, and operations shall be
implemented.

Standards preserve institutional consistency.

Standards reduce ambiguity.

Standards enable interoperability.

Standards transform constitutional principles into
repeatable institutional practice.

6. STANDARDS CHARACTERISTICS

Every COREI standard shall be

identity-aligned,

governance-compliant,

architecture-driven,

engineering-supported,

clear,

unambiguous,

testable,

measurable,

traceable,

versioned,

auditable,

reusable,

and continuously improving.

Standards shall remain technology-independent
unless explicitly defined otherwise.

7. STANDARDS APPLICATION

The Standards Model governs every institutional
standard, including

engineering standards,

platform standards,

security standards,

data standards,

documentation standards,

repository standards,

interface standards,

quality standards,

operational standards,

artificial intelligence standards,

and future institutional standards.

Every standard shall inherit from this model.

8. MODEL DECLARATION

Standards exist to preserve constitutional
consistency throughout the COREI Institution.

They ensure that institutional knowledge,
architecture, engineering, and operations remain
uniform, explainable, interoperable, and
continuously improvable across every generation
of institutional growth.
