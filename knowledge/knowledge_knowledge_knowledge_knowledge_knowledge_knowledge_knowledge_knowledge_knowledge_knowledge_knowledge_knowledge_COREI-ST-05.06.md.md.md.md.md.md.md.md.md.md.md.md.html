COREI DATA STANDARDS

DOCUMENT ID

COREI-ST-05.06

DOCUMENT NAME

DATA STANDARDS

VERSION

1.0

STATUS

APPROVED

CLASSIFICATION

CONSTITUTIONAL

1. PURPOSE

The COREI Data Standards define the constitutional
standards governing every data asset throughout the
COREI Institution.

Their purpose is to ensure that institutional data
remains accurate,
consistent,
trusted,
traceable,
governed,
secure,
interoperable,
and continuously available for every institutional
capability.

Data shall be treated as a constitutional
institutional asset.

2. DATA PHILOSOPHY

Within COREI,

data is not information.

Data is the institutional representation of
observed reality.

Every data element shall possess

meaning,

ownership,

quality,

lineage,

governance,

and institutional purpose.

Technology stores data.

The institution owns data.

3. CONSTITUTIONAL DATA STANDARDS

Every institutional data asset shall comply with
the following constitutional standards.

Standard 1

Institutional Ownership.

Every data asset shall have explicit institutional
ownership.

Standard 2

Single Source of Truth.

Every authoritative business concept shall have
one authoritative source.

Standard 3

Canonical Representation.

Institutional data shall follow canonical models
before implementation-specific formats.

Standard 4

Data Quality.

Every data asset shall maintain measurable quality
through validation and governance.

Standard 5

Data Lineage.

Every data transformation shall remain traceable
from origin to consumption.

Standard 6

Schema Governance.

Every schema shall be versioned,
governed,
documented,
and backward-compatible where appropriate.

Standard 7

Security By Default.

Institutional data shall remain protected
throughout its complete lifecycle.

Standard 8

Interoperability.

Data shall be exchangeable across institutional
platforms through governed contracts.

Standard 9

Auditability.

Every critical data operation shall remain
observable and auditable.

Standard 10

Lifecycle Governance.

Every data asset shall be governed from creation
through archival or retirement.

4. DATA STANDARD CATEGORIES

Data Standards govern

data models,

canonical schemas,

event schemas,

API payloads,

database standards,

streaming data,

market data,

reference data,

master data,

metadata,

data lineage,

data quality,

data retention,

data governance,

and future institutional data standards.

5. DATA RESPONSIBILITIES

Every institutional data asset shall define

business meaning,

institutional ownership,

data steward,

authoritative source,

classification,

quality requirements,

security classification,

retention policy,

lineage,

validation rules,

access policy,

and lifecycle responsibilities.

Data ownership shall never be ambiguous.

6. DATA COMPLIANCE

Every institutional data capability shall
demonstrate compliance with

constitutional Identity,

Governance,

Architecture,

Engineering,

Institutional Standards,

Data Standards,

security requirements,

privacy requirements,

quality requirements,

and operational governance.

Data compliance shall remain measurable and
auditable.

7. DATA VALIDATION

Every institutional data asset shall answer the
following constitutional questions.

Does it have explicit ownership?

Is the authoritative source defined?

Is the schema governed?

Is quality measurable?

Is lineage complete?

Is security implemented?

Can every transformation be audited?

Can consumers trust the data?

Will future institutional generations understand
its meaning?

If any answer is negative,

the data asset shall not be considered
constitutionally compliant.

8. DATA DECLARATION

COREI Data Standards exist to preserve trusted,
consistent, governed institutional data across
every platform, capability, and operational domain.

Every data asset shall strengthen

institutional intelligence,

decision quality,

engineering consistency,

operational reliability,

knowledge preservation,

and long-term institutional continuity.

Trusted institutions are built upon trusted data.

9. AUTHORITY

This document defines the constitutional
Data Standards of COREI.

Every database,

data warehouse,

data lake,

market data platform,

AI dataset,

message schema,

API payload,

event stream,

analytics platform,

reporting platform,

and future institutional data capability

shall comply with these Data Standards.

Changes to this document require constitutional
review.
