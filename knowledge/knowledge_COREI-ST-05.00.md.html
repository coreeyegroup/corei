1. PURPOSE

The COREI Standards System establishes the
constitutional framework governing institutional
standards across the COREI Institution.

Its purpose is to preserve consistency,
interoperability, quality, traceability,
maintainability, and institutional continuity
through every engineering implementation,
platform, system, service, repository,
workflow, and future institutional capability.

Standards transform constitutional principles
into consistent institutional practice.

2. OBJECTIVES

The Standards System exists to

• establish institutional consistency

• preserve architectural fidelity

• reduce engineering ambiguity

• strengthen interoperability

• improve implementation quality

• preserve institutional knowledge

• enable deterministic engineering

• support long-term institutional evolution

3. STANDARDS PHILOSOPHY

Within COREI,

standards are not restrictions.

Standards are institutional agreements.

Standards preserve the consistent expression of
constitutional Identity, Governance,
Architecture, and Engineering throughout every
implementation.

Engineering builds institutional capability.

Standards ensure that institutional capability
remains consistent across generations.

5. STANDARDS RESPONSIBILITY

The Standards Domain owns

institutional standards,

engineering standards,

platform standards,

data standards,

security standards,

documentation standards,

quality standards,

consistency rules,

and standards evolution.

The Standards Domain does not own engineering
implementation or solution specifications.

7. STANDARDS SCOPE

The Standards System governs every institutional
standard across COREI, including

engineering,

platforms,

repositories,

services,

interfaces,

documentation,

No institutional standard exists outside this
constitutional framework.

8. STANDARDS CONTINUITY

The Standards System shall remain stable while
implementation technologies evolve.

Standards shall continuously improve through
institutional learning while preserving
constitutional consistency across every
generation of COREI.

9. AUTHORITY

The COREI Standards System is the authoritative
constitutional standards framework of the COREI
Institution.

Every institutional standard,

engineering standard,

platform standard,

repository standard,

specification,

implementation guide,

and future institutional capability

shall inherit from this Standards System.

Changes to this document require constitutional
review.
