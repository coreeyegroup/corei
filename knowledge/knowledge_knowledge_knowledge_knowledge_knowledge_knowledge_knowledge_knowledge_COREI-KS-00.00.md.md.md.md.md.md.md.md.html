1. PURPOSE

The COREI Knowledge Foundation establishes the
constitutional foundation of the COREI Knowledge System.

It defines the principles, structure, governance,
architecture, standards and operating rules that govern
every Knowledge Asset within the COREI repository.

This foundation exists to ensure that institutional
knowledge remains structured, authoritative,
traceable and continuously reusable across generations.

2. OBJECTIVES

The Knowledge Foundation exists to

• establish one institutional knowledge architecture

• define one authoritative knowledge model

• govern the lifecycle of knowledge

• standardize Knowledge Assets

• eliminate ambiguity

• preserve institutional continuity

• support deterministic engineering

• support artificial intelligence

• prevent knowledge loss

• enable institutional learning

3. DESIGN PRINCIPLES

The COREI Knowledge Foundation follows six
constitutional principles.

  1. Knowledge is an institutional asset.

  2. Knowledge belongs to one authoritative owner.

  3. Knowledge exists in one authoritative location.

  4. Knowledge continuously evolves.

  5. Institutional identity remains stable.

  6. Every Knowledge Asset shall strengthen
    institutional intelligence.

5. KNOWLEDGE DOMAINS

The COREI Knowledge System is organized into
eleven institutional knowledge domains.

01 Identity

02 Governance

03 Architecture

04 Engineering

05 Standards

06 Specifications

07 Operations

08 Implementation

09 Reference

10 History

11 Research

Each domain owns one institutional responsibility.

Responsibilities shall never overlap.

6. KNOWLEDGE OWNERSHIP

Every Knowledge Asset shall define

• Purpose

• Scope

• Ownership

• Authority

• Classification

• Version

• Dependencies

• Review Status

• Repository Location

Every Knowledge Asset shall have exactly one
authoritative owner.

7. KNOWLEDGE LIFECYCLE

Institutional knowledge progresses through the
following lifecycle.

Observation

↓

Discussion

↓

Discovery

↓

Institutional Review

↓

Decision

↓

Knowledge Asset

↓

Repository

↓

Institutional Intelligence

Knowledge shall not remain inside conversations.

Knowledge becomes institutional only after it has
been incorporated into the authoritative repository.

8. KNOWLEDGE GOVERNANCE

The Knowledge Foundation governs the Knowledge System.

Every Knowledge Asset within COREI shall comply with

the Knowledge Model,

the Knowledge Architecture,

the Knowledge Governance Standard,

the Knowledge Lifecycle,

the Knowledge Classification Standard,

the Knowledge Asset Standard,

the Knowledge Naming Standard,

and the Knowledge Repository Standard.

9. AI COMPATIBILITY

The Knowledge Foundation is intentionally designed
to support both human and artificial intelligence.

Every Knowledge Asset shall remain

structured,

deterministic,

machine-readable,

traceable,

version controlled,

and semantically consistent.

The repository shall serve as the authoritative
knowledge source for future AI systems,
engineering tools,
automation platforms,
and operational environments.

10. AUTHORITY

The COREI Knowledge Foundation represents the
constitutional layer of the COREI Knowledge System.

Every future Knowledge Domain,

Knowledge Asset,

architecture,

engineering practice,

platform,

workflow,

AI capability,

and operational system

shall inherit from this foundation.

Changes to this foundation shall require
constitutional review.

This foundation is expected to remain stable
throughout the lifetime of COREI.
