COREI KNOWLEDGE MODEL

DOCUMENT ID

COREI-KS-00.01

DOCUMENT NAME

KNOWLEDGE MODEL

VERSION

1.0

STATUS

APPROVED

CLASSIFICATION

CONSTITUTIONAL

1. PURPOSE

The COREI Knowledge Model defines the institutional
understanding of knowledge.

It establishes what knowledge is,
how knowledge is created,
how knowledge evolves,
how knowledge becomes institutional intelligence,
and how institutional intelligence enables
effective governance and long-term mastery.

Every Knowledge Asset within COREI shall
conform to this model.

2. THE NATURE OF KNOWLEDGE

COREI recognizes that knowledge is not information.

Knowledge is structured understanding.

Information describes reality.

Knowledge explains reality.

Understanding connects reality.

Intelligence enables action.

Governance directs action.

Mastery continuously improves action.

Knowledge therefore represents the bridge
between observation and institutional capability.

3. KNOWLEDGE CONTINUUM

Knowledge develops through a continuous progression.

Reality

↓

Observation

↓

Data

↓

Information

↓

Knowledge

↓

Understanding

↓

Models

↓

Intelligence

↓

Governance

↓

Institutional Capability

↓

Institutional Mastery

Every stage strengthens the next.

No stage shall be omitted.

4. REALITY AND KNOWLEDGE

COREI recognizes that every complex system
contains two interconnected realities.

Visible Reality

and

the Unseen Core.

Visible Reality provides observable evidence.

The Unseen Core explains why
the Visible Reality exists.

Knowledge connects these two realities.

Institutional understanding emerges by
discovering and explaining the Unseen Core.

5. KNOWLEDGE TRANSFORMATION

Knowledge continuously evolves.

Observation creates evidence.

Evidence supports discovery.

Discovery creates explanation.

Explanation becomes knowledge.

Knowledge develops understanding.

Understanding enables models.

Models strengthen intelligence.

Intelligence enables governance.

Governance develops institutional capability.

Institutional capability matures into
institutional mastery.

Knowledge is therefore a living institutional asset.

6. INSTITUTIONAL KNOWLEDGE

COREI distinguishes between
personal knowledge and institutional knowledge.

Personal knowledge exists within individuals.

Institutional knowledge exists independently
of individuals.

Institutional knowledge shall be

Structured.

Version controlled.

Reviewed.

Governed.

Traceable.

Reusable.

Machine-readable.

Continuously evolving.

Institutional knowledge shall remain available
regardless of personnel, technology,
or organizational change.

7. KNOWLEDGE CHARACTERISTICS

Every Knowledge Asset shall possess the following
characteristics.

Accuracy.

Authority.

Consistency.

Clarity.

Traceability.

Reusability.

Completeness.

Maintainability.

Explainability.

Institutional Value.

These characteristics define
high-quality institutional knowledge.

8. KNOWLEDGE RELATIONSHIPS

Knowledge Assets never exist independently.

Each Knowledge Asset may

inherit,

reference,

support,

extend,

constrain,

or govern

other Knowledge Assets.

Relationships shall be explicit,
traceable,
and deterministic.

Knowledge duplication shall be avoided.

9. KNOWLEDGE AS INSTITUTIONAL INTELLIGENCE

Knowledge becomes institutional intelligence
when it enables

understanding,

prediction,

governance,

decision making,

continuous improvement,

and institutional learning.

Institutional intelligence is therefore
knowledge applied with purpose,
discipline,
and governance.

10. KNOWLEDGE MODEL PRINCIPLES

The COREI Knowledge Model follows the
following constitutional principles.

Knowledge is an institutional asset.

Knowledge belongs to one authoritative owner.

Knowledge exists in one authoritative location.

Knowledge continuously evolves.

Knowledge supports institutional intelligence.

Institutional intelligence strengthens governance.

Governance enables institutional mastery.

These principles govern every
Knowledge Asset within COREI.

11. MODEL STATEMENT

The COREI Knowledge Model defines knowledge as
structured institutional understanding that
connects the Visible Reality of a complex system
to its Unseen Core.

Knowledge transforms observation into understanding,
understanding into intelligence,
intelligence into governance,
and governance into enduring institutional capability.

This model shall govern every Knowledge Asset
throughout the COREI Knowledge System.
