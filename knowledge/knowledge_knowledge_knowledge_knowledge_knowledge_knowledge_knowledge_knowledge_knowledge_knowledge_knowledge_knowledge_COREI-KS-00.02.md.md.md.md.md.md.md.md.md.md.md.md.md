# =============================================================================
#
# 1. PURPOSE
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Knowledge System is the institutional operating
system responsible for managing every Knowledge Asset
within COREI.

Its purpose is to transform individual knowledge into
institutional knowledge, preserve that knowledge,
govern its evolution, and make it available to people,
artificial intelligence, automation systems and future
COREI platforms.

The Knowledge System is the authoritative source of
institutional truth.



# =============================================================================
#
# 2. OBJECTIVES
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Knowledge System exists to

• preserve institutional knowledge

• organize institutional knowledge

• govern institutional knowledge

• eliminate knowledge duplication

• ensure one authoritative source of truth

• support institutional decision making

• support AI-native knowledge retrieval

• preserve long-term institutional continuity

• continuously strengthen institutional intelligence



# =============================================================================
#
# 3. SYSTEM PRINCIPLES
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Knowledge System follows the following
constitutional principles.

Knowledge is a strategic institutional asset.

Knowledge shall exist only once.

Knowledge shall have one authoritative owner.

Knowledge shall be discoverable.

Knowledge shall be traceable.

Knowledge shall be governed.

Knowledge shall continuously evolve.

Institutional identity shall remain stable.

Institutional knowledge shall outlive individuals.



# =============================================================================
#
# 4. SYSTEM COMPONENTS
#
# =============================================================================

The COREI Knowledge System consists of four
institutional layers.

Knowledge Foundation

↓

Knowledge Domains

↓

Knowledge Assets

↓

Knowledge Content

Each layer inherits from the layer above it.

Authority flows downward.

Inheritance never flows upward.
