Institutional Engineering Knowledge System

COREI

The institutional knowledge system for understanding, building, operating and continuously evolving COREI.

Core Object
See the unseen core.

This system exists so that a person entering COREI does not need to depend on the original authors to understand the platform. The knowledge system provides the context first, then directs the reader into the detailed engineering material.

What is COREI?

COREI is an institutional trading and technology platform designed to operate across financial markets, instruments, strategies, accounts, companies, operators and infrastructure while preserving deterministic engineering, governance, traceability and continuity.

COREI is not understood by looking at a single application, service, strategy or infrastructure component. The platform must be understood as a connected system.

REALITY ↓ OBSERVATION ↓ UNDERSTANDING ↓ DECISION ↓ ACTION ↓ OBSERVATION OF RESULT ↓ LEARNING / IMPROVEMENT ↺

See the Unseen Core

The visible behaviour of a system is only the surface. COREI investigates what exists underneath that surface.

VISIBLE ↓ UNDERLYING CAUSE ↓ STRUCTURE ↓ STATE ↓ DEPENDENCIES ↓ CONTROL ↓ RISK ↓ FAILURE ↓ RECOVERY ↓ INTELLIGENCE

This principle applies to trading, infrastructure, software, data, execution, operations and institutional knowledge itself.

The objective is not to make every document simple. The objective is to make the route through the knowledge simple. Engineering depth remains inside the appropriate area.

Institutional Working Rules

  • Understand before changing. A component must be understood in its architectural context before implementation begins.
  • Architecture before implementation. Code must be understood as an implementation of an architectural responsibility.
  • Preserve source knowledge. Existing documents are not silently rewritten or discarded during reconstruction.
  • Separate current truth from history. Historical and superseded material remains available but must not masquerade as current architecture.
  • Do not invent missing knowledge. Unknown remains unknown until supported by evidence.
  • See the unseen core. Visible behaviour is never treated as the complete understanding of a system.
